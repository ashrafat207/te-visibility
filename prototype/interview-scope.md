# Prototype scope for interviews

Full version: "Today: what we show at the first interview" in the Build plan tab.
Prototype: https://claude.ai/artifact/S5P4nUdhf27VMxnQbWKhk9 (clickable, seeded data)

## Show
| Screen | Story | Interview task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home / Budget: left today per team and cost centre | 1 | "Find how much is left for [team]." |
| Flags with reasons, incl. one cost-centre miscoding | 3, 4 | "What would you do with this flag?" |
| Scenarios: drop or move a trip, see headroom | 2 | "Cut 15k: what do you drop?" |
| Change log: who changed what, and when | 10 | "Who changed this budget line?" |

## Cut for now
Live agents, real data, spreadsheet import, digest and nudges, forecast, login, traveler quick-log (show only if there's time).

## Before each interview
- [ ] The numbers add up across Home, Budget and Scenarios
- [ ] The miscoding flag and change-log panel are in
- [ ] 3 screens picked, in the order of the guide
