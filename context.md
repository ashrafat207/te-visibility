# Context

_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

## Problem
- Expenses don't show in real time, so no one can say how much of the year's T&E budget is left today.
- People get confused about which expense was for what: little transparency or audit on recent spend.
- Spend is charged to the wrong cost centre, then balanced out elsewhere (the "shell game"). Example: 8k charged to one cost centre, offset by pulling the same amount from another team's or trip's budget.
- Budgets and plans live in several Excel sheets that change without a trace of who changed what, or when.

## ICP
A strategic finance manager or finance lead who owns the T&E budget for several teams that travel globally, with many expenses every week and month.

## Jobs to be done
1. Keep spend under the planned budget, and on forecast to land under or close to it on time.
2. See how much money is in the pot today, so the team knows what it can spend.
3. Plan scenarios for expected spend, so the team can decide which trips to drop or adjust.

## Ideal-case testimonials
- "We saved 2 hours on a busy day making sure T&E was on track, with no unexpected overspend at month end."
- "We fit all our trips into the budget by readjusting and resequencing them."
- "Our team no longer thinks twice about what they can spend."
- "I wish I had this for my own personal or family trips."
